A protein is a chain of amino acids that folds into a specific 3D shape, and that shape determines what it does and which molecules it binds. Protein structure prediction is the task of computing that 3D shape from the amino-acid sequence alone.

Why it matters for drug discovery

Structure-based methods such as [pyxis_learn_link slug="what-is-molecular-docking"]docking[/pyxis_learn_link] need a 3D structure of the target. Experimental methods — X-ray crystallography, cryo-electron microscopy and NMR — are slow and do not work for every protein. Predicted structures fill the gap, covering proteins that have never been solved.

The AI breakthrough

For decades, prediction accuracy lagged far behind experiment. In 2020, DeepMind's AlphaFold2 reached accuracy competitive with experimental structures for many proteins in the CASP14 blind assessment. Its successor, AlphaFold3 (2024), extends prediction to complexes of proteins with DNA, RNA, ions and small-molecule ligands.

OpenFold is an open-source, trainable reproduction of AlphaFold developed by an academic and industry consortium. OpenFold3 continues that work for biomolecular complexes, so researchers and companies can run, inspect and fine-tune the model themselves.

Reading the confidence scores

ScoreMeaning
pLDDT (0–100)Per-residue confidence in local structure. Above ~90 is high confidence; below ~50 often indicates a disordered or flexible region.
PAEPredicted aligned error between pairs of residues, in ångströms. Low values between two domains mean their relative position is reliable.
pTM / ipTMOverall confidence in the fold, and in the interface between chains of a complex.

Limits to keep in mind

  • A prediction usually shows one static conformation; real proteins move, and a [pyxis_learn_link slug="what-is-a-binding-site"]binding pocket[/pyxis_learn_link] may look different with a ligand bound.
  • Side-chain positions inside pockets can be less accurate than the backbone, which affects docking.
  • Predicted ligand poses and complexes should be checked against experimental data where possible.
