# Pyxis Insights editorial guidelines

Each Insights post helps a medicinal or computational chemist understand a
specific development in **macrocycle discovery**. The macrocycle connection
must be stated in the paper's title or abstract; a general drug-discovery story
does not become suitable by adding the word "macrocycles" to the introduction.

## Format

- **Headline:** at most 90 characters, focused on the macrocycle finding.
- **Opening sentence:** explain immediately why this matters for macrocycles.
- **Body:** 150–250 words, normally three short paragraphs. Cover the finding,
  then a practical implication and/or limitation. At most one short heading and
  one helpful Learning Center link. Avoid background that assumes the reader
  wants a biochemistry review, generic sales copy and repeated conclusions.
- **Image:** include an original conceptual macrocycle PNG inline and on the
  Insights card. Its caption must identify it as a conceptual illustration,
  not a molecular structure, predicted complex or experimental figure. An
  editor may replace it with a scientifically relevant, licensed visual.
- **Source:** append the generated citation and paper link, plus the AI note.

## Scientific and editorial checks

Use only facts from the selected title and abstract. Never imply Pyxis took
part in a paper. Do not turn model predictions into measured findings,
preliminary hits into validated leads, or a binding prediction into proof of
molecular-glue activity. Preserve all caveats needed to understand the finding.
Every numeric claim must occur in the source; leave uncertain details out. An
exact source DOI is treated as an identifier, never as evidence for a numeric
scientific claim.

A post with no eligible source is skipped. Length, opening, HTML, links and
numeric checks reject bad drafts before WordPress. These checks cannot prove
scientific correctness: a person must read the source and candidate, check the
macrocycle connection and limitations, and approve its image before publishing.
The AI note must never claim a human review has happened automatically.

Reviewers publish from WordPress after approval. Generated candidates remain
in Draft or Pending Review; email/phone delivery depends on separately configured
notifications.
